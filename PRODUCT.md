# Product

## Register

brand

The public site (landing, menu, events, reservations) is a brand surface where design IS the product. The admin CMS is a product surface where design SERVES the tool. This file carries brand as the primary register; admin tasks may override per-command.

## Users

**Restaurant guests:** Montreal locals and tourists seeking authentic Brazilian cuisine and nightlife. Context: browsing the menu, checking events, booking a table. Mobile-first, often on-the-go.

**Admin staff:** Restaurant owners and managers updating menu items, monitoring reservations, and publishing events. Desktop-focused, needs efficiency and clarity.

## Product Purpose

Boteco Montreal brings the soul of Brazilian botecos from Rio and São Paulo to the heart of Quebec. The website exists to:
- Showcase the authentic menu and cultural programming
- Convert visitors into diners through easy reservations
- Provide staff with tools to manage content without engineering help

## Brand Personality

Tropical, sofisticado, ritmado (vibrant, sophisticated, rhythmic). "Tropical Professional" — Brazilian energy with Montreal sophistication. Confident, warm, culturally rich. Speaks French primarily, with Portuguese and English as complementary voices.

## Anti-references

- Generic corporate SaaS templates (Inter font, purple-to-blue gradients, rounded-square icon tiles above every heading)
- Overly minimal "clean" restaurant sites that strip all personality
- Cards nested inside cards
- Gray text on colored backgrounds
- Bounce/elastic easing animations

## Design Principles

1. **Rhythm as identity** — spacing, motion, and layout should pulse like samba. Not chaotic, but alive.
2. **Authenticity over trend** — Brazilian cultural references (Copacabana pattern, warm wood tones, tropical greens) should feel earned, not appliquéd.
3. **Clarity under richness** — vibrant colors and bold typography must never sacrifice legibility or usability.
4. **Mobile is the bar** — most guests arrive on phone; the mobile experience sets the standard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text contrast)
- Supports French, Portuguese, and English content
- Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Touch targets ≥ 44px on mobile
